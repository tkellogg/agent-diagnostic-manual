# Diagnostic foundations

## Four distinct claims

| Claim | Example | What would support it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation | The same information was retrieved repeatedly. | Calls, source versions, and returned content. |
| Descriptive diagnosis | Retrieval repeated with little information gain and impaired progress. | A trace meeting explicit criteria, after considering lookalikes. |
| Mechanism | Compaction repeatedly removed necessary evidence. | Model-visible inputs and controlled comparisons that distinguish alternatives. |
| Intervention effect | Preserving evidence improved completion. | Measured outcomes under stated comparison conditions. |

These claims can have different strengths in the same case. A name for a pattern is not itself a causal explanation.

## What we borrow from diagnostic practice

The APA describes DSM entries as explicit criteria accompanied by material on course, functioning, differential diagnosis, and co-occurring conditions. Its history describes DSM-III's attempt to remain neutral about causes. We borrow the discipline of defining and distinguishing patterns; our agent categories are our own proposals.

A **differential diagnosis** is the process of comparing plausible explanations and gathering evidence that separates them. It can include ordinary behavior, environmental failure, and multiple interacting causes. A symptom alone does not determine a diagnosis.

## Three standards

- **Reliability:** reviewers applying the definition reach similar judgments.
- **Validity:** independent evidence supports the category as a meaningful distinction.
- **Utility:** the category improves investigation or decisions.

A label may satisfy one standard without satisfying the others. Two reviewers agreeing on a label does not establish its mechanism. A successful intervention does not by itself establish the category's validity.

## System boundary

Assess an episode of a configured agent. Record model/version, harness/version, tools, instructions, context policy, and environment. A model may correctly obey a rule that makes the whole system behave poorly. Keep responsibility and mechanism separate from the observed system-level failure.

Prefer traces of inputs, outputs, and state transitions over an agent's narrative about why it acted. An explanation generated by the agent is evidence to assess, not privileged access to causation.

## Sources and limits

- [APA: About DSM](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/dsm/about-dsm) — diagnostic criteria, descriptive material, and reliability.
- [APA: DSM history](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/dsm/about-dsm/history-of-the-dsm) — the move toward explicit criteria and relative causal neutrality.
- [MedlinePlus: Differential diagnosis](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/differential-diagnosis/) — comparing explanations and choosing discriminating investigations.
- [NIMH: About RDoC](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/research/research-funded-by-nimh/rdoc/about-rdoc) — limitations of sharp categories and the role of dimensions and mechanisms.

These sources motivate the method. They do not validate any ASM agent diagnosis or intervention. All operational definitions in the first entries are proposals.
